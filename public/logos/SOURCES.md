# Platform logo assets

X, Telegram, Apple, OpenAI, Outlook, Instagram, WhatsApp and Facebook use the SVG paths from Simple Icons 10.0.0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/10.0.0/icons . The CC0 project license is included as SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt. Only the root fill color was set to match the display surface; logo geometry is unchanged.

The multicolor Google G is from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Google_%22G%22_logo.svg (Google LLC; public-domain simple logo geometry). Original asset: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c1/Google_%22G%22_logo.svg .

Logos identify the products' platforms. Trademarks belong to their respective owners; their appearance does not imply endorsement or affiliation.

All assets are served locally without third-party image requests.
